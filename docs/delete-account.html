Delete your PARALLEL account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open PARALLEL and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Privacy and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: without the app

Email us from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted: your login, profile answers, life snapshots and scenarios, future-self stories, daily cards, plans, tasks, check-ins, XP, streaks and badges.

What we may keep: nothing tied to you in our app database. Our providers' backups are cleared on their normal schedules, usually within 30 days. Google Play keeps its own purchase records as required by law.

Have PARALLEL Plus? Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in the Google Play Store app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
