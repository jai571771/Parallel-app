PARALLEL

Meet the life you didn't choose.

PARALLEL helps you explore possible versions of your future, understand the trade-offs behind your choices, and turn the path you prefer into a simple plan. One tiny What-If a day, a friendly firefly called Pip, and your own branching life map.

Possibilities, not predictions. Every scenario in PARALLEL is an illustration based on what you tell us and the assumptions we show you. It is not a forecast, and it is not professional, financial or medical advice.
