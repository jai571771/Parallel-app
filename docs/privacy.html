Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

PARALLEL ("we", "us") is an app that helps you imagine possible versions of your future. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, email jai571771@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, an optional first name, and your password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Things you tell us: your onboarding answers, such as life stage, age range, ambitions, interests, habits, strengths, challenges, the time you have available, and the decisions you want to explore. You can skip any question.
  • Things the app creates for you: life snapshots and scenarios, future-self stories, daily What-If cards and your answers to them, action plans, tasks, and check-in notes.
  • Progress and settings: XP, streaks, badges, daily reminder time, your device's time zone, and how many AI explorations you have used this month.
  • Subscription status: whether you have PARALLEL Plus and when it renews or ends. Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

Why we use it

  • To create your personalised scenarios, stories, daily cards and plans.
  • To save your progress so you can come back to it, and (if you allow "scenario memory") to remind you of paths you explored.
  • To run your account, enforce free-plan limits and unlock Plus features you paid for.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Who helps us run PARALLEL

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and server functions. Your account and app data are stored here.
  • Google (Gemini API): to write scenarios, stories, cards and plans, we send the relevant answers from your profile and your chosen decision to Google's Gemini AI. Google processes this under its API terms.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: to process and verify PARALLEL Plus subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID and your purchase history in our app.

These providers only use your data to provide their service to us.

Sharing

Your scenarios are private by default. They only leave the app if you share a card, and share cards only include path titles and short taglines, never your profile answers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete it any time:

  • In the app: Profile → "Delete all my data" (keeps your login) or "Delete my account" (removes everything, immediately).
  • Without the app: see Delete your account.

Backups held by our providers are cleared on their normal schedules, usually within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Profile → "Export my data" gives you a copy of everything as a file.
  • Correct: edit your answers any time in Profile.
  • Delete: as described above.
  • Scenario memory and reminders: turn them on or off in Profile.

Children

PARALLEL is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Not advice

PARALLEL creates hypothetical, assumption-based scenarios. It is not a therapist, financial adviser or scientifically validated forecasting tool. For health, money or mental-wellbeing decisions, please talk to a qualified professional.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and each user can only access their own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Email jai571771@gmail.com.
